# Roomba+ 4.5.0b1

**The first beta of 4.5.0.** Everything in it is listed in
[4.5.0](v4.5.0.md); this page is the short version and what to test.

Needs *Show beta versions* in HACS. Built on **roombapy-prime 0.7.1**,
as 4.4.4 is. Upgrade notes: [UPGRADING](../docs/UPGRADING.md#v450--from-v44x).

---

## Maintenance, rebuilt

- **One status for every part and care task**, on Classic and Prime:
  `sensor.<robot>_maintenance` (ok, soon, due, overdue) and
  `event.<robot>_maintenance`. The remaining hours follow the series and
  iRobot's counters on every cloud poll.
- **The maintenance list on both generations**, open only when
  something is due. **Prime items can be ticked off**: the reset goes to
  iRobot the way the app sends it.
- **Configure → Maintenance**: an overview, all settings in one place,
  "report a part replaced", "forget learned intervals" and the care
  guides.
- **Care guides** for every part and care task, written and drawn for
  Roomba+ in eight languages, in the variant of your robot: the filter,
  brushes, corner brush and caster as your series has them, the
  contacts on your dock. The part is highlighted on a drawing of your
  robot and dock. Linked from the list, the calendar, the event, the
  repair issues and the vacuum while an error, a refusal to start or a
  full bin points to a care task. The link opens without signing in.
- **Opt-in**: a maintenance calendar, a repair issue per part a week
  overdue, and the care guide as a repair issue while an error lasts.
- **A new installation starts lean**; an existing one keeps its
  entities and can hide the diagnostic ones.

## Blueprints

- **New: Maintenance** -- a notification per part or task when it comes
  due, within the hours you choose, with the care guide and **Replaced**
  / **Cleaned**, **Only cleaned**, **Remind me later** and **How to do
  it** buttons.
- **Four blueprints made automations Home Assistant refused to load**
  (Notifications, Vacuum then mop, Smart start on away, Demand clean
  alert). Fixed -- re-import them.
- **Exception notifications rewritten**; set its automations up again.
  New in it: a notification when iRobot's numbers, arriving minutes
  after a Classic mission, show it as unusual -- a lot of dirt, perhaps
  a spill (`roomba_plus_mission_reassessed`). The never raised "Possible
  floor accident detected" repair issue is gone.

## Rooms

- **900-series: rooms from the robot's own runs**, with straight walls,
  doors where it actually drove through, and a carried robot no longer
  taken for drift. Smart Map and Prime: walls and doors on the rooms map.
- **Japanese, Chinese and Korean room names** on the maps instead of
  boxes, with a font you put into `roomba_plus/fonts` in the
  configuration folder (TROUBLESHOOTING says which and where).
- Rooms a mission was only sent to are marked as such; sending the robot
  home leaves the room; mission names by map and type.

## What to test

1. **Prime robots: tick off a part.** In the maintenance list, or
   Configure → Maintenance → *Report a part replaced*. The counter should
   go back to full here and in the iRobot app. If iRobot does not apply
   it, Home Assistant says so -- please send the message and a
   diagnostics download.
2. **A care guide link** from the maintenance list, in the browser and
   in the companion app: does the page open, with the drawing of your
   robot? Does it look right in dark mode?
3. **The maintenance blueprint** with a phone notification: do the
   buttons tick the part off, and does "How to do it" open the guide?
4. **Your robot's guide**: does it describe your filter, brushes and
   dock the way they are? The texts per series come from iRobot's care
   articles, not from every model in hand.
5. **Your language**: the care texts are new in all eight languages.
   Corrections are welcome.
6. Anything that reads differently from 4.4.4 in the remaining hours --
   with a diagnostics download (`maintenance` shows where each interval
   comes from).
7. **Classic robots with the iRobot login:** after a very dirty run,
   does `roomba_plus_mission_reassessed` arrive (Developer tools →
   Events, or the Exception notifications blueprint)? And does it stay
   quiet after ordinary runs?
