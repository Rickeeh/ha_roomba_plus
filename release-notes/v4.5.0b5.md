# Roomba+ 4.5.0b5

**The fifth beta of 4.5.0.** Everything in 4.5.0 is listed in
[4.5.0](v4.5.0.md); this page is what changed since
[4.5.0b4](v4.5.0b4.md).

Needs *Show beta versions* in HACS. Built on **roombapy-prime 0.8.2**,
as b4.

---

## Fixed

- **The rooms under the cleaning path, and the room map, empty after a
  restart** (@pk-1966, i7+, after updating to the beta; back after
  returning to 4.4.4). A Smart Map robot's map with its room outlines is
  read from the iRobot cloud when Roomba+ starts. When that request
  failed, or the answer had no outlines, nothing asked again until the
  next mission ended or a day later, and the failure was logged at debug
  level only. Until then the cleaning path stood on a blank background,
  and room tracking and the room map had no rooms either.
  - Roomba+ now asks again every **10 minutes**, up to six times in a
    row; a map that really has none then waits for the daily refresh or
    the next mission, so the cloud is not kept busy.
  - The log says it once, at **info** level: that the outlines did not
    load, and when they did.
