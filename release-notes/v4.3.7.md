# Roomba+ 4.3.7

**Skipping the current room is sent as the iRobot app sends it, with
the mission's map and the room, and a skipped room counts as done for
the end of the mission.**

---

## Skip, as the app sends it (#178)

A tester's S9+ took `skip` from 4.3.4 and cleaned on: the robot recorded
the command and stayed in its room. A look into how the iRobot app
(7.18.0) does it explains why. The app does not send a bare `skip` but a
mission command:

```json
{"command": "skip", "pmap_id": "…", "user_pmapv_id": "…",
 "regions": [{"region_id": "<the room>", "type": "rid"}],
 "ordered": 0, "params": {"reschedule": false}}
```

Roomba+ sent only `{"command": "skip"}`, without map or room. A robot
that drops such a command without a word fits what the S9+ did; it is
not yet confirmed on a robot.

**On Classic robots, `vacuum.send_command` with `command: skip` now
sends what the app sends:**

- the map and map version of the command that started the mission
  (kept since 4.3.5, also across a restart since 4.3.6);
- the room the robot is in, from Roomba+'s room tracking, or the room
  you name: `params: {room: Kitchen}` (a name or an id of the mission's
  rooms);
- zones go out as zones (`zid`).

**It is offered where the app offers it, and refused otherwise with the
reason:**

- the robot has to offer it (`cap.mc` 2 or 3);
- the robot has to be cleaning or driving between rooms (phase `run`),
  not paused, charging or on its way home;
- it has to be a room mission started with its map;
- the room has to be known. Room tracking counts only in the order the
  mission was sent; for a mission without an order, name the room.

**The last room of a mission sends the robot home** instead (`dock`):
nothing is left to clean, and the Prime app does the same.

**Prime robots** keep the plain `skip` their app sends. Its initiator
stays `localApp`, like every other command Roomba+ sends; the Prime app
says `rmtApp`, and nothing so far shows the robot treating the two
differently.

The diagnostics (`field_evidence.skip_attempts`) record what was sent,
how the room was found (tracked or named, position in the plan) and the
mission 20 seconds and a minute later. The app waits up to 20 seconds
for the room to change.

## A skipped room counts as done at mission end

The app shows a room event with status 7 or 8 as skipped. Roomba+ did
not know these values, so a mission with a skipped room waited out the
full time limit of its end-of-mission check instead of being confirmed
by the cloud. A skipped room now closes like a finished one there. It
still does not count as cleaned in the room history, unless the robot
covered floor in it before.

## Under the hood

- `IRobotVacuum._classic_skip_command()`: the checks and the payload;
  `_skip_room_index()`: the tracked room, only where the held start was
  ordered and the plan has its length, or the named one. Refusals:
  `skip_not_supported`, `skip_not_cleaning`, `skip_not_room_mission`,
  `skip_room_unknown`, in all eight languages.
- `skip_attempts` entries gain `sent` (`skip` or `dock`), `room`
  (`source`, `index`, `of`, `last_room`; no ids) and `after_20s`.
- `const.ROOM_EVENT_SKIPPED_STATUSES` = {7, 8}, part of
  `ROOM_EVENT_CLOSED_AT_END_STATUSES`.
